title: "Globus Maintenance Downtime on October 24"
excerpt: Globus services will be down on Saturday, starting at 7:30 AM, for about an hour.  Websites will be down and transfers paused.
---

Globus has planned a maintenance downtime!  Globus services will be down
starting at 7:30 AM (US/Pacific time) on Saturday, October 24.  Globus services
will be down for up to one hour.

You can read the full details of what's affected on [Globus' blog
post](https://www.globus.org/blog/2026-10-24-maintenance).  From our
perspective, here is what the downtime means:

* All transfers will be paused at the start of the downtime.  This includes
  transfers to or from Stanford, the cloud, or your laptop.

* The Globus web site will be unavailable, so you won't be able to start new
  transfers or do things like directory listings.

* The Globus CLI will stop working during the downtime.

* Programs which Globus will not work.  For example, elm-archive will not
  be able to start new transfers during the downtime.  You should avoid
  launching new elm-archive runs until after the downtime.

* Websites which use Globus might not work.  For example, the Stanford Digital
  Repository self-deposit site may not work properly.

If you experience problems after the downtime is complete, please let Globus
Support know.  You can email Globus support at
[support@globus.org](mailto:support@globus.org).
